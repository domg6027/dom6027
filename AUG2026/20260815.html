📖 MESSAGE

The Torah teaches that human life carries a unique dignity because humanity was created in the image of Elohim. This truth establishes a foundation for justice: to harm human life is not merely to injure another person, but to violate something that bears the imprint of the Creator.

Bereshith 9:6 connects the seriousness of shedding human blood directly to the image of Elohim. The value of human life does not depend upon wealth, status, nationality, usefulness or social approval. Every human being bears that image, and therefore justice must recognise the inherent dignity of human life.

The history recorded in Melakhim Bet shows what happens when leaders and nations repeatedly disregard YHVH's warnings. Prophets were sent to call the people back to obedience, but rebellion, idolatry and injustice continued. Eventually judgement came upon both Yisrael and Yehudah.

The prophetic warnings were therefore acts of mercy as well as declarations of judgement. YHVH did not conceal the consequences of persistent rebellion. He warned before judgement came, giving people opportunities to turn back to Him.

These two themes belong together. When human beings are created in the image of Elohim, justice cannot be separated from reverence for the Creator. And when YHVH sends prophetic warning, His people are responsible for hearing and responding rather than assuming that judgement will never come.

💙 APPLICATION

Examine how you regard the people around you this Shabbat. Do you recognise the image of Elohim even in those with whom you strongly disagree? Do your words and actions preserve their dignity, or do anger, prejudice and contempt cause you to treat them as less than human?

Also examine how you respond to warning. The history of Melakhim Bet reminds us that repeated warnings should not be ignored simply because judgement has not yet arrived. YHVH's patience is an opportunity for repentance, not permission for continued rebellion.

Let us therefore honour the image of Elohim in others, take injustice seriously, listen when YHVH's Word corrects us and remain humble enough to change direction when we are warned.

👐 CLOSING PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for creating humanity in Your image and for teaching us to value life, justice and righteousness. Forgive us where we have treated others with contempt, prejudice or indifference. Teach us to recognise the dignity You have placed within every human being and to act justly toward those around us.

We pray for peace in the Middle East, South Africa and throughout the world. In this time of continued tension and uncertainty, especially in and around Yisrael and its neighbouring nations, bring restraint where conflict threatens to grow, grant wisdom to leaders, protect innocent lives and establish Your righteousness and lasting peace according to Your perfect will.

We pray for an end-time revival. Awaken hearts across every nation, call people to repentance, restore a deep love for Your truth and raise up faithful servants who will proclaim the Good News of Your Kingdom with boldness and humility.

We pray for the elderly, especially those who suffer from loneliness, neglect or abuse. Surround them with loving families, compassionate caregivers and faithful believers who will honour them with dignity, kindness and practical support.

We pray for farmers and all who labour to provide food for their communities. Protect them from violence, strengthen them through every hardship, bless the work of their hands and grant favourable seasons that will produce abundant harvests.

We pray for those affected by wars, natural disasters and humanitarian crises. Comfort those who grieve, heal the injured, provide for the displaced and restore hope to those whose lives have been shaken by suffering and loss.

We pray for evangelists, teachers, missionaries and all faithful servants who proclaim Your truth throughout the world. Strengthen them with wisdom, courage and endurance, protect them where persecution is increasing and open doors for the Good News to reach many hearts.

We pray for those who are ill, especially those battling cancer, chronic diseases and emerging viral outbreaks. Stretch out Your healing hand, guide those who provide medical care, strengthen families walking alongside them and fill every heart with hope and peace.

We pray concerning the coming tribulation and the signs unfolding before our eyes. Grant Your people discernment, steadfast faith and spiritual maturity. Help us to remain watchful, prepared and unwavering as we await the return of Yahusha HaMashiach.

We lift up Derech Olam Ministries before You. Direct every decision according to Your perfect will, provide every resource needed for the work You have entrusted to us, open doors that no one can shut, bless every ministry platform and let Your Name alone receive all honour and glory.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
