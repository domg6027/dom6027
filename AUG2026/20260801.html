📖 MESSAGE

The Torah's instructions concerning warfare reveal that YHVH did not present conflict as the first response. Before a city was attacked, an offer of peace was to be proclaimed. Only when that offer was rejected and the opposing city chose war did the process of siege follow. This demonstrates that even within the difficult reality of warfare, there were boundaries intended to restrain unnecessary conflict and establish a lawful path toward peace.

The account of Shelomoh shows another dimension of righteous leadership. When YHVH offered him the opportunity to ask for what he desired, Shelomoh asked for an understanding heart so that he could judge YHVH's people rightly and distinguish between good and evil. His request pleased YHVH because he sought wisdom for faithful service rather than personal wealth, long life or power.

Yet the history of Shelomoh also gives us a serious warning. Wisdom itself does not guarantee lifelong faithfulness. Despite receiving extraordinary wisdom and building the Temple, Shelomoh's heart was eventually drawn away through his relationships with foreign women and their worship. His beginning demonstrates the beauty of wisdom and devotion, while his later decline demonstrates the danger of allowing divided loyalties to undermine covenant faithfulness.

These themes belong together. Righteous leadership requires both wisdom and restraint. Whether dealing with nations in conflict or making decisions within our own lives, YHVH calls us to seek peace where possible, discern what is right and remain devoted to Him rather than allowing power, desire or influence to redirect our hearts.

As followers of Yahusha HaMashiach, we are likewise called to pursue peace without abandoning truth, to seek wisdom rather than impulsive reactions and to guard our hearts against anything that competes with our devotion to YHVH. Peace is not weakness, wisdom is not merely knowledge, and devotion cannot be sustained by yesterday's faithfulness. Each day requires a renewed commitment to walk in the ways of YHVH.

💙 APPLICATION

Examine your own approach to conflict this Shabbat. Do you seek peaceful resolution before confrontation whenever righteousness allows it? When you must make difficult decisions, do you seek wisdom from YHVH before relying on your own understanding?

Also examine your heart for divided loyalties. Shelomoh's life reminds us that even great wisdom can be undermined when devotion to YHVH is gradually compromised. Choose today to guard your heart, pursue peace, seek righteous wisdom and remain wholly devoted to YHVH.

👐 CLOSING PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for teaching us to pursue peace, wisdom and faithful devotion. Give us discernment to know when peace can be offered, courage to uphold righteousness when conflict cannot be avoided, and wisdom to make decisions that honour You. Guard our hearts from divided loyalties and from anything that could draw us away from Your covenant. May we remain humble, teachable and steadfast, following the example of faithful obedience and keeping our hearts devoted to You through Yahusha HaMashiach.

We pray for peace in the Middle East, South Africa and throughout the world. In this time of continued tension and uncertainty, especially in and around Yisrael and its neighbouring nations, bring restraint where conflict threatens to grow, grant wisdom to leaders, protect innocent lives and establish Your righteousness and lasting peace according to Your perfect will.

We pray for an end-time revival. Awaken hearts across every nation, call people to repentance, restore a deep love for Your truth and raise up faithful servants who will proclaim the Good News of Your Kingdom with boldness and humility.

We pray for the elderly, especially those who suffer from loneliness, neglect or abuse. Surround them with loving families, compassionate caregivers and faithful believers who will honour them with dignity, kindness and practical support.

We pray for farmers and all who labour to provide food for their communities. Protect them from violence, strengthen them through every hardship, bless the work of their hands and grant favourable seasons that will produce abundant harvests.

We pray for those affected by wars, natural disasters and humanitarian crises. Comfort those who grieve, heal the injured, provide for the displaced and restore hope to those whose lives have been shaken by suffering and loss.

We pray for evangelists, teachers, missionaries and all faithful servants who proclaim Your truth throughout the world. Strengthen them with wisdom, courage and endurance, protect them where persecution is increasing and open doors for the Good News to reach many hearts.

We pray for those who are ill, especially those battling cancer, chronic diseases and emerging viral outbreaks. Stretch out Your healing hand, guide those who provide medical care, strengthen families walking alongside them and fill every heart with hope and peace.

We pray concerning the coming tribulation and the signs unfolding before our eyes. Grant Your people discernment, steadfast faith and spiritual maturity. Help us to remain watchful, prepared and unwavering as we await the return of Yahusha HaMashiach.

We lift up Derech Olam Ministries before You. Direct every decision according to Your perfect will, provide every resource needed for the work You have entrusted to us, open doors that no one can shut, bless every ministry platform and let Your Name alone receive all honour and glory.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
