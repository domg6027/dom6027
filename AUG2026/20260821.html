⭐ THEME

Justice - Liberty under Law

📘 READING

“You shall do no injustice in judgment. You shall not be partial to the poor nor honour the person of the mighty. In righteousness you shall judge your neighbour.”
- Vayiqra (Leviticus) 19:15

“Thus says YHVH: Execute judgment and righteousness, and deliver the plundered out of the hand of the oppressor.”
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 22:3

“For the Torah of freedom is the perfect Torah, and whoever looks into it and continues in it, being not a forgetful hearer but a doer of the work, this one shall be blessed in what he does.”
- Ya'aqov (James) 1:25

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH's Torah establishes liberty within the boundaries of righteous law. Justice does not mean that every person may simply do whatever seems right in his own eyes. True liberty exists within an order where the rights, dignity and responsibilities of others are protected.

The command to judge without partiality shows that justice must not be controlled by wealth, poverty, status or influence. The oppressed must be protected, while those who exercise authority must remain accountable to the same righteous standard.

The Renewed Covenant describes the Torah as the Torah of freedom. This freedom is not lawlessness; it is the freedom to live rightly under YHVH's instruction. Liberty under law therefore protects people from both oppression by others and the destructive consequences of rejecting righteous boundaries.

🩵 REFLECTION

Freedom without justice can quickly become freedom for the strong to take advantage of the weak. Likewise, law without justice can become a tool of oppression. YHVH's way joins liberty and righteousness together.

True liberty therefore requires boundaries. We are free to make choices, but our choices do not give us permission to harm, exploit or oppress others. YHVH's righteous law protects the dignity and freedom of each person while holding everyone accountable for how that freedom is exercised.

May we never confuse liberty with lawlessness. May we value the protection that righteous boundaries provide, defend those who are vulnerable to injustice, and remain willing to submit our own choices to YHVH's standard. Let our freedom be guided by justice, our justice by truth, and our lives by obedience to YHVH.

👐 PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for giving us righteous instruction that protects both freedom and justice. Teach us to understand liberty according to Your ways rather than according to human selfishness.

Keep us from partiality, oppression and the misuse of authority. Give us courage to defend those who are treated unjustly and wisdom to recognise when freedom is being used as an excuse for harming others.

Help us to live as people who are free to choose what is right while remaining accountable to Your Torah. Teach us to respect the freedom and dignity of others without surrendering truth or righteousness.

May our communities be shaped by justice, our freedom protected by righteous boundaries, and our choices guided by the fear of YHVH. Help us to practise Your Torah faithfully, not merely hearing it but living it.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
