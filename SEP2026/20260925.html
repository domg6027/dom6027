⭐ THEME

Prohibition of relations during niddah

📘 READING

“You shall not approach a woman to uncover her nakedness as long as she is put apart for her customary impurity.”
- Vayiqra (Leviticus) 18:19

“If a man lies with a woman during her sickness and uncovers her nakedness, he has exposed her flow, and she has uncovered the flow of her blood. Both of them shall be cut off from their people.”
- Vayiqra (Leviticus) 20:18

“Therefore, having these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of Elohim.”
- Qorintiyim Bet (2 Corinthians) 7:1

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH established boundaries around intimacy that protect the holiness of the body, the dignity of the woman, and the order He placed within human relationships. The prohibition concerning niddah is therefore not merely a restriction on physical activity, but an instruction to recognise and respect a condition that YHVH has specifically addressed in Torah.

What is niddah? Niddah refers to the period of menstrual impurity described in Torah, beginning with a woman's menstrual bleeding and continuing for the prescribed period. During this time, YHVH commands specific boundaries concerning physical intimacy, teaching His people to recognise and respect the appointed separation rather than treating it as something to ignore or conceal.

Obedience requires restraint even within a relationship where affection, desire, and mutual commitment are present. The existence of a legitimate marital relationship does not remove the boundaries established by YHVH. Instead, Torah teaches that intimacy itself must remain subject to His instructions.

Respect for niddah also requires patience and consideration. A boundary established by YHVH should never become an occasion for pressure, shame, manipulation, or contempt. Faithfulness means accepting His instruction and allowing His order to govern our conduct, even when personal desire might urge us in another direction.

🩵 REFLECTION

Holiness is demonstrated not only by what we desire, but by our willingness to restrain ourselves according to the instructions of YHVH. The ability to wait is part of obedience, and self-control is an expression of honour toward both YHVH and the person with whom we share a covenant relationship.

There are times when obedience requires us to place the word of YHVH above immediate desire. Such restraint is not a rejection of intimacy, but a recognition that intimacy has boundaries established by the Creator. His instructions teach us that what is permitted at one time is not necessarily permitted at every time.

Let us therefore examine our understanding of obedience. Do we respect the boundaries YHVH has established even when they require patience? Do we treat His instructions as expressions of His wisdom, or do we allow personal desire to negotiate with His word? May our conduct demonstrate reverence, self-control, dignity, and faithfulness.

👐 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, You are the Creator and the One who establishes the boundaries of holiness. Teach us to honour Your instructions concerning the body, intimacy, marriage, and the seasons You have established.

Give us self-control when obedience requires restraint. Remove impatience, selfishness, pressure, or manipulation from our relationships, and teach us to treat one another with dignity and honour. Help us to recognise that Your boundaries are not burdensome when our hearts are submitted to You.

Guard our relationships from anything that would violate Your Torah. Give us wisdom to understand Your instructions, humility to obey them, and love that expresses itself through patience, respect, and faithfulness. May our private conduct be as honouring to You as our public words.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
