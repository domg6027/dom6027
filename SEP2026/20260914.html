🍷 COMMUNION

We remember Yahusha HaMashiach and the covenant represented through His body and blood. As we partake, let us remember that covenant calls us beyond outward profession into a life of obedience, faithfulness and transformation.

Purity of conduct begins with recognising that our bodies, relationships and choices are not outside the authority of YHVH. Let this remembrance lead us to examine the ways in which our conduct reflects the covenant we have received. May we not excuse impurity simply because it has become common around us. May we instead allow the truth of YHVH to cleanse our thinking, correct our behaviour and strengthen our commitment to walk faithfully before Him.

📖 MESSAGE

Purity of conduct is not merely about avoiding a list of prohibited actions. It is about recognising that our entire manner of living belongs under the authority of YHVH. Torah does not leave holiness as an abstract ideal. It gives practical boundaries that teach us how to live differently from the surrounding nations.

Leviticus 18 begins by establishing this distinction clearly. YHVH warns His people not to imitate the practices of Egypt or Kena'an. The issue is therefore larger than individual behaviour. The way a people conducts itself reveals whose standards it has accepted.

This remains a serious challenge. A society can normalise behaviour without making that behaviour righteous. Repetition does not transform disobedience into obedience. Popular acceptance does not establish Torah. Cultural pressure does not cancel the boundaries YHVH has given.

Purity therefore requires discernment. We must learn to recognise when the standards around us have begun to influence our own thinking. Sometimes compromise enters gradually. Something once recognised as wrong becomes familiar, then tolerated, then defended. Eventually the conscience can become so accustomed to it that correction feels offensive rather than necessary.

YHVH's Torah protects us from that gradual corruption. His boundaries remind us that human relationships are not disposable and that our bodies are not merely instruments for satisfying every desire. Sexual conduct carries consequences because it involves covenant, trust, family, dignity and the proper order established by YHVH.

Purity also requires personal responsibility. It is easy to identify corruption in the world while overlooking it within ourselves. Yom Teru'ah calls us to awaken, and awakening includes allowing YHVH's instruction to examine our own conduct.

Where have we accepted something because everyone else accepts it? Where have we allowed desire to become more authoritative than obedience? Where have we defended conduct that Torah requires us to abandon? Where have we become more concerned about cultural approval than faithfulness to YHVH?

The answer is not despair. The answer is repentance and obedience. When YHVH reveals impurity, He gives us the opportunity to turn away from it. Purity is not achieved by pretending that temptation does not exist. It is pursued by recognising temptation, refusing compromise and choosing obedience.

May the second day of Yom Teru'ah strengthen this awakening within us. May we become increasingly sensitive to what defiles, increasingly determined to reject compromise and increasingly faithful in every private and public area of our lives.

🌙 YOM TERU'AH DAY 2: Awakening and kingship

Yom Teru'ah Day 2 continues the call to awaken under the kingship of YHVH. The appointed time reminds us that YHVH's authority is not limited to our worship gatherings or public declarations. His kingship reaches into our relationships, our bodies, our thoughts and our conduct.

To awaken is to become attentive again. It means recognising where our lives have drifted and refusing to treat disobedience as harmless simply because it has become familiar.

YHVH is King, and His Torah teaches His people how to live under His authority. The boundaries He establishes are not arbitrary restrictions. They protect what He has declared sacred and preserve the order He established for His people.

As we continue through Yom Teru'ah, may the call to awakening become increasingly personal. May we listen when correction comes, respond when truth exposes compromise and return quickly when we recognise that we have wandered. May this appointed time remind us that true awakening produces action. We are not merely called to hear the teru'ah; we are called to respond to the King.

🤲🏻 CLOSING PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for bringing us into Yom Teru'ah Day 2. Thank You for continuing to awaken our hearts and for giving us Your Torah as a faithful guide for our conduct. Search us and reveal every area where impurity, compromise or cultural influence has weakened our obedience. Give us discernment to recognise what is contrary to Your ways and courage to remove it from our lives.

Protect our bodies, relationships, marriages, families and communities. Keep us from sexual immorality and from allowing the standards of the surrounding nations to replace Your instruction. Teach us to honour the boundaries You have established and to understand that purity protects dignity, covenant and life.

Give us strength when temptation comes. Help us distinguish between what we desire and what You permit, between what society approves and what You command, and between temporary pleasure and lasting righteousness.

We pray for those who are struggling with temptation, those who have fallen into sexual sin, those carrying the consequences of past choices, and those who genuinely desire to return to righteous conduct. Lead them into truth, repentance and restoration.

We pray for our families and communities, that they may be protected from corruption and strengthened in purity, faithfulness and righteousness.

We pray for those who are suffering, discouraged or spiritually weary. Renew their strength and awaken hope within them. Help them to continue walking faithfully even when the journey is difficult.

As we continue through the appointed times of the seventh month, keep us attentive to Your voice. Let Yom Teru'ah continue to awaken our conscience and remind us that You are King over every part of our lives. May our conduct honour You in public and in private. May our repentance be genuine, our choices disciplined and our obedience steadfast.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
