⭐ THEME

Justice – order through separation

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH established an order for His people through His instructions and Right-Rulings. Wayyiqra makes it clear that obedience is not optional: Yisra’el was commanded to guard His Laws, refuse the practices of the nations that defiled them, and recognise the distinction YHVH had established.

This separation was not intended to create injustice or give anyone permission to mistreat others. It was a covenantal distinction that identified those who belonged to YHVH and were called to live according to His standards. He separated His people to be His own, and their conduct was to reflect that calling.

The Renewed Covenant reinforces this principle. Qorin’tiyim 2 warns against forming unions that compromise righteousness and against allowing darkness to become joined with the light. The instruction to come out and be separate is connected directly to our identity as the Mishkan of the living Elohim.

Justice requires order, and order requires that the boundaries established by YHVH be respected. When we disregard His distinctions, we open the way to confusion, compromise and practices that oppose His instructions. At the same time, we must not invent divisions or use separation as an excuse for pride, prejudice or unrighteous treatment of others.

Our responsibility is to remain faithful to YHVH, to examine the influences we allow into our lives, and to ensure that our relationships, choices and conduct do not draw us away from His Word.

We are called to belong to Him, to walk according to His order and to demonstrate through our lives that we are His people.

🩵 REFLECTION

Do I guard the instructions and Right-Rulings of YHVH, or do I allow the standards of the world to influence my decisions?

Are there relationships, agreements or practices in my life that compromise my obedience to YHVH?

Do I understand the difference between being set apart for YHVH and treating others unjustly?

Have I allowed human traditions or personal preferences to create divisions that YHVH Himself has not commanded?

Separation is not merely about what we reject. It is also about whom we belong to and the standards by which we choose to live.

May we respect the order established by YHVH, remain faithful to His covenant and walk as a people set apart for Him.

🤲🏻 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, thank You for calling us to belong to You and for establishing the order by which we are to live. Teach us to guard Your instructions and respect the distinctions You have established.

Give us discernment to recognise influences that lead us away from Your truth. Help us to separate ourselves from unrighteousness without becoming proud, prejudiced or unjust towards others. Examine our relationships, decisions and commitments, and show us where we need to make changes.

Strengthen us through Your Ru’ach ha’Qodesh to remain faithful to Your covenant, to walk in righteousness and to reflect Your character in our daily lives. May our conduct demonstrate that we are Your people and that our loyalty belongs to You.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
