THEME

Justice – honor of the Name

READING

“And you shall not profane My set-apart Name, but I shall be set-apart among the children of Yisra’el. I am YHVH who sets you apart.”
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 22:32

“And I shall set apart My great Name, which has been profaned among the nations, which you have profaned in their midst. And the nations shall know that I am YHVH,” declares the Master YHVH, “when I am set apart in you before their eyes.”
- Yehezqel (Ezekiel) 36:23

“Having your behavior among the nations good, so that when they speak against you as evil-doers, they shall, by observing your good works, esteem Elohim in a day of visitation.”
- 1 Kepha (1 Peter) 2:12

MITSVOT FOCUS

Yesterday we considered the responsibility of guarding reverent speech. Today we continue by examining how justice and righteous conduct honour the Name of YHVH. The Name is not honoured merely by speaking it correctly or by avoiding its misuse. We honour His Name when our lives reflect the righteousness, truth and compassion that He commands.

The mitzvah to sanctify the Name calls us to live in a way that distinguishes what is set apart from what is common. YHVH’s Name must never be used to justify injustice, dishonesty or the mistreatment of others. When we claim to represent Him, our conduct becomes part of the testimony people see. If our actions contradict His instructions, we bring dishonour to the very Name we profess to honour.

Justice is therefore an essential expression of reverence. We honour YHVH when we deal honestly, fulfil our responsibilities, keep our commitments and refuse to exploit those who are vulnerable. We honour Him when we judge fairly, acknowledge wrongdoing and make restitution where we have caused harm. Righteousness must be visible in our dealings, not merely declared by our lips.

The words of Yehezqel remind us that YHVH’s Name is made known among the nations through His people. Our conduct can either obscure His character or help others recognise His truth. We must never present personal opinions, anger or prejudice as though they were the instructions of YHVH. Those who speak in His Name carry a serious responsibility to distinguish His Word from their own understanding.

Kepha teaches that good conduct can become a testimony even among those who misunderstand or speak against us. We cannot control every accusation, but we can remain faithful in how we respond. We should not seek to defend the Name through arrogance, hostility or unrighteous actions. Instead, let our obedience, integrity and compassion demonstrate the truth we proclaim.

As we prepare to enter Shabbat, let us examine whether our lives honour the Name of YHVH in our homes, our relationships, our work and our communities. May our conduct be just, our words truthful and our hearts humble. Let us enter the day of rest with a renewed commitment to sanctify His Name through the way we walk.

REFLECTION

Do our actions honour the Name of YHVH, or do they contradict the righteousness we profess?

Are we fair and truthful in our dealings with others, especially when we have authority or when someone is vulnerable?

When we speak in the Name of YHVH, do we carefully distinguish His instructions from our own opinions and emotions?

As we prepare for Shabbat, let us consider what needs to be corrected, restored or made right. May our lives become a faithful testimony to the holiness and justice of YHVH.

PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, we thank You for teaching us to honour Your set-apart Name through righteous conduct. Help us to understand that reverence is not only expressed through our words, but also through our choices, responsibilities and treatment of others.

Search our hearts and reveal every way in which our conduct has brought dishonour to Your Name. Forgive us for injustice, dishonesty, pride, prejudice or neglect. Give us the humility to acknowledge our wrongdoing and the courage to make right what we have damaged.

Guide us by Your Ru’ach HaQodesh. Teach us to act justly, to speak truthfully and to fulfil our commitments with integrity. Keep us from using Your Name to defend our own anger, opinions or desires. Help us to represent Your instructions faithfully and to walk in the righteousness You require.

As we prepare for Shabbat, cleanse our hearts and establish peace within our homes and communities. May our conduct among the nations be good, and may those who observe our lives see a testimony of Your justice, mercy and truth.

May Your great Name be sanctified in us. Let our lives honour You in all things, and strengthen us to walk faithfully in Your ways.

In the Name of Yahusha HaMashiach, we pray.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
