THEME

Prohibition of blasphemy

READING

“You do not bring the Name of YHVH your Elohim to naught, for YHVH does not leave him unpunished who brings His Name to naught.”
- Shemot (Exodus) 20:7

“And he who blasphemes the Name of YHVH shall certainly be put to death.”
- Vayiqra (Leviticus) 24:16

“With it we bless our Elohim and Father, and with it we curse men, who have been made in the likeness of Elohim. Out of the same mouth proceed blessing and cursing. My brothers, this should not be so.”
- Ya'aqob (James) 3:9–10

MITSVOT FOCUS

The Name of YHVH is set apart. It is not an ordinary word, a casual expression or a tool to strengthen our arguments. When YHVH commanded His people not to bring His Name to naught, He was teaching them to treat His identity, authority and reputation with reverence. The commandment calls us to examine not only what we say, but also how we represent Him through our words and conduct.

Blasphemy is more than using the Divine Name as an exclamation or speaking irreverently. It includes treating what is set apart as common, speaking contemptuously against YHVH, falsely attributing our own words or desires to Him, and using His Name to justify what He has not commanded. When we claim that YHVH has said something He has not said, we place our own words under His authority and misrepresent His character. This is a serious matter.

The prohibition also calls us to guard against careless speech about the things of YHVH. We should not use His Name to manipulate others, threaten them with invented judgments, or make our personal opinions appear to be Divine instructions. Nor should we use the Name to cover dishonesty, cruelty or injustice. A person may speak the Name frequently while failing to honour the One whose Name is spoken.

Reverence is not merely a matter of pronunciation. We should honour the Name in our speech, but we must also honour it in our choices. If we speak of YHVH as righteous while acting unjustly, or proclaim His truth while deliberately deceiving others, our conduct contradicts the words coming from our mouths. We bring dishonour to the Name when our lives misrepresent His character.

The words of Ya'aqob remind us that our mouths can be used for blessing and for harm. We cannot sincerely praise Elohim while deliberately cursing, humiliating or degrading people who bear His image. This does not mean that we must remain silent about wrongdoing or avoid righteous correction. It means that even when we speak firmly, our words must be governed by truth, justice and self-control rather than contempt or hatred.

We must also be careful when correcting others. Reverence for the Name does not give us permission to speak arrogantly, shame people unnecessarily or claim authority that YHVH has not entrusted to us. We should correct error with humility, distinguish Scripture from our own interpretation, and be willing to acknowledge when we have spoken wrongly. A person who fears YHVH will not use His Name to protect personal pride.

Let us therefore guard the Name of YHVH with both our lips and our lives. Let our words be truthful, our claims about Him be responsible, and our conduct reflect the righteousness we proclaim. May we never treat the set-apart Name as common, nor use it to excuse what is contrary to His instructions. Our speech should honour YHVH and bring blessing rather than dishonour and harm.

REFLECTION

Do we treat the Name of YHVH as set apart, or have we allowed it to become casual, careless or merely customary in our speech?

Have we ever claimed that YHVH said something when we were actually expressing our own opinion, desire or interpretation? Are we willing to correct ourselves when we have misrepresented Him?

Do our words honour the people who bear the image of Elohim, even when we must speak truthfully about wrongdoing? Can we correct others without contempt, cruelty or pride?

Let us examine our speech today. May we honour the Name of YHVH not only by what we say about Him, but also by the truthfulness, reverence and righteousness of the lives we live before Him.

PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, we honour Your set-apart Name and thank You for teaching us to treat it with reverence. Forgive us for every careless word, every false claim and every occasion on which our speech has misrepresented Your character.

Teach us to speak truthfully about You. Guard us from using Your Name to justify our own desires, opinions or actions. Give us discernment to distinguish Your instructions from our interpretations, and humility to acknowledge when we have spoken wrongly.

Purify our speech from contempt, manipulation, dishonesty and pride. Help us to correct error with wisdom and to stand for truth without becoming cruel or unjust. Remind us that the people around us bear Your image and that our words must not become instruments of hatred or humiliation.

May our mouths be used to bless, encourage, teach and proclaim what is true. Let our conduct agree with the words we speak about You. Keep us from bringing Your Name to naught through hypocrisy, and help us to honour You in every decision and every conversation.

We ask that the Ru'ach HaQodesh guide our thoughts, guard our tongues and strengthen our obedience. May the Name of YHVH be honoured through our speech and through the righteousness of our lives.

In the Name of Yahusha HaMashiach, we pray.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
