Prohibition of wearing mixed garments (shatnez)

- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 19:19

“Do not put on a garment of different kinds, of wool and linen together.
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 22:11

“Do not become unevenly yoked with unbelievers. For what partnership has righteousness and lawlessness? And what fellowship has light with darkness?

- Qorin’tiyim 2 (2 Corinthians) 6:14–16

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH gave Yisra’el clear instructions concerning mixtures: livestock of different kinds were not to be crossbred, fields were not to be sown with mixed seed, and garments woven from different kinds of thread were not to be worn. Devarim specifically identifies wool and linen together.

These instructions teach us that YHVH establishes distinctions and expects His people to respect what He has set apart. We are not at liberty to disregard His instructions simply because a mixture appears convenient or attractive to us. The command concerning garments must first be understood as a practical Torah instruction. We should take care to understand and obey what YHVH actually commanded, rather than replacing His instruction with our own interpretations.

The Renewed Covenant reading also reminds us that our spiritual lives must not be joined in compromising partnerships. We cannot unite righteousness with lawlessness, light with darkness, or the Mishkan of Elohim with idols. Our loyalty belongs to YHVH.

🩵 REFLECTION

Do I take YHVH's instructions seriously, even when they concern ordinary, everyday matters such as clothing?

Do I distinguish between what YHVH has commanded and what people have added through tradition or personal interpretation?

Are there relationships, partnerships, influences or practices in my life that compromise my loyalty to YHVH?

The command concerning mixed garments gives us an opportunity to examine how carefully we respect YHVH's distinctions. Let us not mix obedience with compromise, truth with deception, or faithfulness with disloyalty.

May our conduct, choices and relationships reflect an undivided commitment to YHVH.

🤲🏻 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, thank You for Your instructions and for teaching us to respect the distinctions You have established. Give us understanding to recognise what You have commanded and the willingness to obey without adding to or taking away from Your Word.

Examine our hearts and expose every area where we have allowed compromise to enter. Help us to separate ourselves from unrighteousness, deception and idolatry. Keep our loyalty firmly established in You and guide our daily choices so that our lives reflect Your truth.

Strengthen us through Your Ru’ach ha’Qodesh to walk faithfully in Your ways, with discernment, integrity and obedience.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
