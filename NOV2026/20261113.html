📨 DAVAR LECHEM – Erev Shabbat, Tishi'i 3, 6028 AA

Shalom, Mitspacha!

⭐ THEME

Prohibition of cross-dressing

📘 READING

“A woman does not wear that which pertains to a man, nor does a man put on a woman's garment, for whoever does this is an abomination to YHVH your Elohim.”
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 22:5

“And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you prove what is that good and well-pleasing and perfect desire of Elohim.”
- Romiyim (Romans) 12:2

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH created humanity with distinction, purpose and order. In Devarim 22:5, He gives a clear instruction concerning the distinction between male and female clothing. A woman is not to wear what pertains to a man, and a man is not to put on a woman's garment.

Clothing can communicate identity, presentation and roles. Our outward conduct should therefore reflect respect for the instructions of YHVH.

We must also understand this command responsibly. Clothing styles and customs differ across cultures and throughout history. We should not assume that every garment carries the same meaning in every place or period, nor should we invent additional prohibitions that YHVH did not give.

Romiyim 12:2 calls us to transformation through the renewing of our minds. This transformation includes our choices, conduct and outward presentation. We are not to allow the standards of the world to replace the instructions of YHVH.

Obedience begins in the heart and becomes visible in how we live. This command must never become an excuse to ridicule, humiliate or mistreat others. We are called to examine ourselves, walk humbly and remain faithful to the Word of YHVH.

🩵 REFLECTION

Do I respect the distinction between male and female established by YHVH, or do I treat His order as something that can be disregarded?

Do my choices concerning clothing and outward presentation reflect respect for His instructions?

Have I allowed cultural trends or personal preferences to take precedence over YHVH's standards?

Do I distinguish between YHVH's command and additional rules created by human traditions?

Am I willing to examine my own conduct before judging others?

May we honour YHVH through our choices, allow His Word to guide our conduct and remain faithful to the order He established.

🤲🏻 PRAYER

YHVH, our Elohim, thank You for the order and distinction You established in creation and for the instructions You gave us. Grant us understanding to respect Your commands without adding human traditions or replacing Your standards with the customs of the world.

Give us discernment to examine our own choices, including our outward presentation. Renew our minds through Your Word and strengthen us through Your Ru'ach ha'Qodesh to walk in obedience, humility and integrity.

Guard us against pride, judgement and the mistreatment of others. Help us to remain faithful to Your instructions and to honour You in all that we do.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
