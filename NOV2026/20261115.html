⭐ THEME

Justice – clarity of identity

📘 READING

“And Elohim created the man in His image, in the image of Elohim He created him – male and female He created them.
- Berĕshith (Genesis) 1:27

“A woman does not wear that which pertains to a man, nor does a man put on a woman's garment, for whoever does this is an abomination to YHVH your Elohim.
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 22:5

“There is not Jew nor Greek, there is not slave nor free, there is not male and female, for you are all one in Messiah Yahusha.
- Galatiyim (Galatians) 3:28

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH created humanity with distinction and purpose. Male and female are not accidental categories or identities invented by society. They are part of the order established by the Creator. Recognising this distinction is not an act of injustice; it is an acknowledgement of the design of Elohim.

Torah gives instruction concerning the distinction between male and female, including the prohibition against deliberately adopting the clothing associated with the opposite sex. We must distinguish between what YHVH commands and what cultures, traditions and changing social expectations impose. Justice requires that we remain faithful to the instruction of YHVH rather than allowing human customs to replace His standards.

At the same time, Galatiyim 3:28 teaches that distinctions of identity do not establish unequal covenant worth. In Yahusha HaMashiach, men and women stand together as one in the promise. Justice therefore rejects prejudice, contempt and the mistreatment of anyone. We must uphold the created order without denying the dignity and worth of every person before Elohim.

🩵 REFLECTION

Do I recognise the distinction YHVH established when He created male and female?

Do I distinguish between the commands of Torah and customs introduced by human society?

Do I uphold the instructions of YHVH even when the surrounding culture challenges them?

Do I treat men and women with equal dignity and fairness while respecting their created distinctions?

Does my understanding of justice come from the Word of YHVH, or am I allowing human opinion to determine what I believe?

True justice requires both clarity and integrity. We cannot claim to honour the Creator while disregarding His design, nor can we claim to uphold His justice while treating another person with contempt. Our responsibility is to remain faithful to His Word and to demonstrate fairness, humility and respect in our conduct.

🤲🏻 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, Creator of all things, we acknowledge Your wisdom and the order You established from the beginning. Teach us to recognise Your design and to respect the distinctions You created. Keep us from allowing the opinions and expectations of the world to replace Your instructions.

Give us discernment to distinguish between Your commandments and the traditions of men. Help us to study Your Word carefully, to speak truthfully and to act with integrity. Let our understanding of justice be shaped by Your righteousness rather than by prejudice, fear or personal preference.

Teach us to uphold the dignity of every person without compromising the truth You have given us. Remove arrogance, hostility and unfair judgement from our hearts. Help us to walk in humility, treating others with fairness while remaining obedient to Your Torah.

Strengthen us through Your Ru'ach ha'Qodesh to live faithfully in the example of Yahusha HaMashiach. May our words, choices and actions reflect the justice and truth of Your Kingdom.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
