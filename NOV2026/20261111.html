⭐ THEME

Maintaining holy distinctions

📘 READING

“For I am YHVH your Elohim, and you shall qadosh yourselves. And you shall be qodesh, for I am qodesh. And do not defile yourselves with any creeping creature that creeps on the earth.
‘For I am YHVH who is bringing you up out of the land of Mitsrayim, to be your Elohim. And you shall be qodesh, for I am qodesh.
‘This is the Torah of the beasts and the birds and every living creature that moves in the waters, and of every creature that creeps on the earth, to make a distinction between the unclean and the clean, and between the living creature that is eaten and the living creature that is not eaten.’
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 11:44–47

“Therefore, having girded up the loins of your mind, being sober, put your expectation perfectly upon the favour that is to be brought to you at the revelation of Yahusha HaMashiach, as obedient children, not conforming yourselves to the former lusts in your ignorance, instead, as the One who called you is qodesh, so you also should become qodesh in all behaviour, because it has been written, ‘Be qodesh, for I am qodesh.’
And if you call on the Father, who without partiality judges according to each one’s work, pass the time of your sojourning in reverence.
- Kĕpha 1 (1 Peter) 1:13–17

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH repeatedly instructs His people to recognise and maintain the distinctions He has established. Wayyiqra teaches that the distinction between clean and unclean is not a human invention or a matter of personal preference. YHVH Himself gave these instructions and connected them directly to His own holiness.

The command to be qodesh means that YHVH's people are to be set apart for Him. This is not merely an outward identity or a label we claim. It must be reflected in our conduct, our choices and our willingness to follow His instructions.

Kĕpha reminds us that being set apart must extend into every area of our behaviour. We are not to return to the former desires and practices that shaped our lives before we came to understand the truth. Our expectation in Yahusha HaMashiach must be accompanied by obedience, reverence and a transformed way of living.

Maintaining holy distinctions also requires discernment. We must distinguish YHVH's actual instructions from human traditions, personal preferences and teachings that have no foundation in His Word. We must neither blur the boundaries YHVH has established nor invent boundaries He has not commanded.

Our calling is to live as a people who belong to YHVH, demonstrating through our daily conduct that we recognise His authority and respect His holiness.

🩵 REFLECTION

Do I recognise the distinctions YHVH has established, or do I allow convenience and personal preference to influence my obedience?

Does being set apart for YHVH show in my everyday conduct, or is it only something I profess?

Have I examined the traditions and teachings I follow to determine whether they agree with YHVH's Word?

Am I careful not to add to His instructions or disregard what He has commanded?

Being qodesh is a daily commitment. It calls us to examine our choices, separate ourselves from what defiles us, and remain faithful to the instructions of YHVH.

May our lives demonstrate that we belong to Him, not merely through what we say, but through how we walk.

🤲🏻 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, thank You for calling us to be set apart for You. Teach us to recognise and maintain the distinctions You have established in Your Word. Give us discernment to distinguish Your instructions from human traditions and personal interpretations.

Examine our hearts and our conduct. Show us where we have blurred the boundaries You established, where we have compromised, or where we have followed teachings without testing them against Your Word.

Strengthen us through Your Ru’ach ha’Qodesh to walk in holiness, obedience and reverence. May our thoughts, words, choices and actions reflect our calling as Your people. Help us to remain faithful to You in every area of our lives.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
