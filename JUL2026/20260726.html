📨 DAVAR LECHEM – Yom Rishon, Chamishi 11, 6028 AA

Shalom, Mitspacha!

⭐ THEME

Active Responsibility for Others

📘 READING

“You shall not stand idly by while your neighbour's blood is shed. I am YHVH.”
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 19:16

“You shall love your neighbour as yourself. I am YHVH.”
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 19:18

“Let each of you look out not only for his own interests, but also for the interests of others.”
- Pilipiyim (Philippians) 2:4

🟫 MITZVOT FOCUS

YHVH calls His people to take an active interest in the wellbeing of others. His commandments extend beyond avoiding wrongdoing; they require us to act with compassion, justice and practical care whenever our neighbour is in need. To ignore suffering when we have the ability to help is inconsistent with the love that YHVH commands.

This mitzvah reminds us that covenant faithfulness is expressed through responsible action. Whether offering encouragement, providing practical assistance or protecting someone from harm, we honour YHVH when we willingly serve those around us.

🩵 REFLECTION

Our daily lives present countless opportunities to reflect the compassion of YHVH. A kind word, a helping hand or a willingness to stand alongside someone in difficulty may become the very means through which YHVH brings hope and encouragement.

May we never become indifferent to the needs of others. As we actively seek the wellbeing of our neighbours, our lives become living testimonies of the love, justice and mercy of YHVH.

👐 PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for calling us to care for one another with compassion and faithfulness. Open our eyes to recognise the needs around us, give us willing hearts to serve without hesitation and grant us wisdom to act with love and discernment. May our lives reflect Your kindness, justice and mercy as we faithfully care for those You place in our path.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.

Shalom until tomorrow.
