📨 DAVAR LECHEM – Yom Sheni, Chamishi 12, 6028 AA

Shalom, Mitspacha!

⭐ THEME

Justice - Intervention over Indifference

📘 READING

“You shall not stand idly by while your neighbour's blood is shed. I am YHVH.”
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 19:16

“Open your mouth, judge righteously, and defend the cause of the poor and needy.”
- Mishlei (Proverbs) 31:9

“Therefore, to him who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.”
- Ya'aqov (James) 4:17

🟫 MITZVOT FOCUS

YHVH calls His people to reject indifference and to act whenever justice, mercy and truth require our involvement. His Torah teaches that righteousness is demonstrated not only by avoiding evil but also by taking responsibility to protect, defend and support those who are vulnerable. Remaining passive when we have the opportunity to do good falls short of the standard that YHVH desires.

This mitzvah reminds us that obedience is active rather than passive. YHVH entrusts us with opportunities to bring encouragement, defend the oppressed and uphold righteousness in practical ways that honour His holy Name.

🩵 REFLECTION

Indifference allows injustice to continue, while compassionate action reflects the heart of YHVH. Whether through speaking truth, offering practical help or standing beside someone in difficulty, every act of faithful obedience becomes a testimony of His Kingdom.

May we never ignore opportunities to do what is right. As we choose courage over comfort and compassion over convenience, YHVH uses our lives to bring hope, healing and encouragement to those around us.

👐 PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for calling us to be people who act with compassion, justice and courage. Keep us from becoming indifferent to the needs of others, and give us discernment to recognise the opportunities You place before us each day. Strengthen us to defend what is right, to serve with humility and to reflect Your love through faithful obedience. May our lives honour You and point others toward Your righteous Kingdom.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.

Shalom until tomorrow.
