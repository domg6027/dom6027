📨 DAVAR LECHEM – Yom Shlishi, Chamishi 13, 6028 AA

Shalom, Mitspacha!

⭐ THEME

Prohibition of Standing Idly by Bloodshed

📘 READING

“You shall not stand idly by while your neighbour's blood is shed. I am YHVH.”
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 19:16

“Rescue those being led away to death, and hold back those staggering toward slaughter.”
- Mishlei (Proverbs) 24:11

“Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfil the Torah of Messiah.”
- Galatiyim (Galatians) 6:2

🟫 MITZVOT FOCUS

YHVH forbids His people from remaining passive when another person's life is threatened. This command reaches beyond physical violence to include every situation where timely intervention, protection or assistance can preserve life or prevent harm. Love for our neighbour is demonstrated through responsible action rather than silent observation.

This mitzvah reminds us that covenant obedience includes the courage to step forward when others are in need. Whether by offering practical help, speaking truth, interceding in prayer or protecting the vulnerable, we honour YHVH when we refuse to ignore opportunities to do good.

🩵 REFLECTION

The world often teaches people to look away from another person's suffering, assuming that someone else will act. YHVH calls His people to a different standard. He desires hearts that notice, care and respond with wisdom, compassion and courage.

May we never become spectators when YHVH places someone in our path who needs our help. As we choose faithful action over comfortable silence, our lives become living testimonies of His love, justice and mercy.

👐 PRAYER

Heavenly Father, YHVH, thank You for teaching us to value every human life and to respond with compassion when others are in need. Give us eyes to recognise opportunities to help, wisdom to act according to Your will and courage to overcome fear or indifference. May we faithfully protect, encourage and serve those You place before us, so that our lives may reflect Your righteousness, mercy and love.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.

Shalom until tomorrow.
