📨 DAVAR LECHEM – Yom Sheni, Shmini 8, 6028 AA

Shalom, Mitspacha!

⭐ THEME

Prohibition of sorcery

📘 READING

“There shall not be found among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, or one who practices divination, or a soothsayer, or one who interprets omens, or a sorcerer.”
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:10

“Do not turn to mediums or spiritists; do not seek them out, to be defiled by them. I am YHVH your Elohim.”
- Vayiqra (Leviticus) 19:31

“Many also of those who had practiced magic brought their books together and burned them in the sight of all. And they counted up the value of them, and it came to fifty thousand pieces of silver. So the Word of YHVH grew mightily and prevailed.”
- Ma’asei (Acts) 19:19–20

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH forbids sorcery, divination and the seeking of hidden knowledge or spiritual power through practices He has not authorised. These practices are not harmless entertainment when they invite people to seek guidance or control through forbidden spiritual means. YHVH calls His people to trust Him and to walk in obedience to His instructions.

The desire to know the future, influence events or gain power over others can make forbidden practices appear attractive. Yet curiosity does not make disobedience acceptable. We must not seek answers through mediums, spiritists, spells, charms or other practices that claim access to supernatural knowledge or power apart from YHVH.

We must also exercise discernment when such practices are presented as harmless traditions, cultural customs, games or alternative forms of spirituality. A practice does not become righteous merely because it is popular, familiar or described as light-hearted. We are responsible for examining what we participate in and what we encourage others to accept.

Repentance requires more than recognising that a practice is forbidden. As seen in Acts, those who turned away from sorcery openly abandoned the practices associated with it. We, too, must be willing to turn away from anything that competes with our loyalty to YHVH and His Word.

🩵 REFLECTION

Do I ever seek hidden knowledge or certainty through practices that YHVH has forbidden?

Have I accepted any spiritual practice simply because it is presented as entertainment, tradition or harmless curiosity?

When I feel uncertain about the future, do I turn first to YHVH, His Word and righteous counsel?

Is there anything I need to reject or abandon to bring my conduct into agreement with YHVH’s instructions?

👐 PRAYER

YHVH, our Elohim, teach us to walk faithfully in Your ways and to reject every practice that You have forbidden. Guard our hearts against the desire for hidden knowledge, control or spiritual power apart from You.

Give us discernment to recognise sorcery and divination even when they are disguised as entertainment, tradition or harmless curiosity. Help us to test what we encounter against Your Word rather than allowing popularity or human opinion to determine what is right.

When fear of the future or uncertainty tempts us to seek forbidden guidance, remind us to turn to You in prayer, to study Your instructions and to seek righteous counsel. Strengthen us to trust Your wisdom without demanding knowledge of everything that lies ahead.

Where we have participated in practices that displease You, grant us sincere repentance and the courage to abandon them completely. Help us to remove their influence from our homes and lives and to guide others with truth, patience and compassion.

Keep our communities faithful to Your Torah. May we seek wisdom without superstition, guidance without deception and spiritual understanding through obedience to Your Word. Let our lives demonstrate wholehearted loyalty to You.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
