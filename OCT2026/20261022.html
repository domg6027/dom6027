⭐ THEME

Prohibition of divination

📘 READING

“There shall not be found among you anyone who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, or one who practises divination, or a user of magic, or one who interprets omens, or a sorcerer, or one who conjures spells, or a medium, or a spiritist, or one who calls up the dead. For whoever does these is an abomination to YHVH.”
– Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:10–12

“Do not eat meat with the blood still in it. Do not practise divination or soothsaying.”
– Vayiqra (Leviticus) 19:26

– Acts 16:19

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH forbids divination because His people are called to seek guidance through Him and to walk according to His instructions. Divination attempts to uncover hidden knowledge, predict events or gain direction through practices that He has prohibited. Such practices may appear harmless, fascinating or helpful, but they direct our trust away from YHVH and toward sources that do not honour His authority.

Divination can take many forms, including fortune-telling, interpreting omens, consulting mediums and using spiritual practices to obtain secret knowledge about the future. These practices should not be confused with the wisdom, discernment and understanding that come from learning and obeying the Word of YHVH. Seeking His guidance requires humility, patience, prayer and a willingness to accept His instructions rather than demand knowledge of what lies ahead.

The desire to know the future often grows out of fear, uncertainty or the wish to control circumstances. Yet hidden knowledge cannot replace faithful obedience. YHVH has given us His instructions to guide our conduct, teach us what is right and help us make responsible decisions. We do not need forbidden spiritual practices to determine how to walk faithfully today.

We must also exercise discernment when such practices are presented as entertainment, cultural tradition or harmless curiosity. A practice does not become acceptable merely because it is popular or because someone claims that it produces accurate results. We are called to examine what we encounter against the instructions of YHVH, reject what He forbids and help others understand the difference between genuine wisdom and forbidden spiritual seeking.

🩵 REFLECTION

Am I seeking hidden knowledge or trying to predict the future through any practice that YHVH forbids?

When I feel uncertain or afraid, do I turn to YHVH and His instructions, or seek control through forbidden spiritual practices?

Have I accepted any form of divination because it was presented as entertainment, tradition or harmless curiosity?

What practical step can I take today to strengthen my trust in YHVH and follow His guidance faithfully?

👐 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, give us discernment to recognize and reject divination and every forbidden practice that draws our trust away from You. Forgive us where curiosity, fear or the desire to control the future has led us toward what You have prohibited. Teach us to seek wisdom through Your Word and to walk faithfully in the instructions You have given us.

Strengthen our trust when the way ahead is uncertain. Help us resist deception, test what we encounter against Your truth and encourage others to choose obedience over forbidden spiritual seeking. May our decisions honour You and reflect our confidence in Your wisdom and authority.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
