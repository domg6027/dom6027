⭐ THEME

Prohibition of necromancy

📘 READING

“Do not turn to mediums, and do not seek after spiritists, to be defiled by them. I am YHVH your Elohim.”
- Wayyiqra (Leviticus) 19:31

“Let no one be found among you who makes his son or his daughter pass through the fire, or one who practices divination, or a user of magic, or one who interprets omens,
or a sorcerer, or one who conjures spells, or a medium, or a spiritist, or one who calls up the dead.
For whoever does these is an abomination to YHVH.”
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:10–12

“And when they say to you, ‘Seek those who are mediums and spiritists, who whisper and mutter,’ should not a people seek their Elohim? Should they seek the dead on behalf of the living? To the Torah and to the witness!
If they do not speak according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.”
- Yesha’yahu (Isaiah) 8:19–20

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH forbids His people from seeking guidance through mediums, spiritists, or those who claim to communicate with the dead. Such practices turn people away from trusting Him and seeking wisdom through His instruction. The prohibition is not merely about avoiding certain rituals; it calls us to remain faithful to YHVH as the One to whom we turn for truth, guidance, and understanding.

The desire to contact the dead may arise from grief, fear, curiosity, or a longing for answers. Yet our emotions and unanswered questions do not make forbidden practices acceptable. YHVH knows our sorrow and our need for direction. We can bring our questions, burdens, and grief before Him without crossing the boundaries He has established.

Discernment also requires us to recognise that not every spiritual claim comes from a trustworthy source. Claims of hidden knowledge, messages from the dead, or supernatural insight must not replace the Torah or become an authority over our lives. We must test what we hear against YHVH’s instruction and reject practices that draw us away from obedience.

Our responsibility is to seek the living Elohim, uphold His boundaries, and help others understand the difference between genuine trust in Him and forbidden attempts to obtain secret knowledge. We honour YHVH when we choose obedience over curiosity and His truth over spiritual deception.

🩵 REFLECTION

Where might grief, fear, or curiosity tempt someone to seek answers through forbidden spiritual practices?

How can I respond when someone presents a spiritual claim that contradicts YHVH’s instruction?

Am I seeking guidance through YHVH’s Word, or am I being drawn toward sources that promise hidden knowledge?

How can I encourage someone who is grieving or searching for answers without compromising the boundaries established by YHVH?

👐 PRAYER

YHVH our Elohim, we thank You that You are the source of truth, wisdom, and righteous guidance. Forgive us for every time we have sought answers in places that draw us away from Your instruction. Cleanse our hearts of curiosity that disregards Your boundaries, and strengthen us to remain faithful to You.

Give us discernment to recognise spiritual deception and the courage to reject every practice that You have forbidden. When we face grief, uncertainty, fear, or unanswered questions, teach us to bring these burdens before You rather than seeking forbidden sources of knowledge.

Help us to comfort those who mourn, guide those who are confused, and speak Your truth with compassion and clarity. May our lives demonstrate that we trust You, honour Your Torah, and refuse to exchange Your light for deceptive promises.

Guard our families and communities from spiritual deception. Lead us in Your truth, and help us walk faithfully in the path You have established.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
