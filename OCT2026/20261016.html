⭐ THEME

Prohibition of Listening to False Prophets

📘 READING

“If a prophet or a dreamer of dreams arises among you, and he gives you a sign or a wonder, and the sign or the wonder comes to pass, of which he spoke to you, saying, ‘Let’s go after other gods’ (which you have not known) ‘and let’s serve them,’ you shall not listen to the words of that prophet, or to that dreamer of dreams; for YHVH your Elohim is testing you, to know whether you love YHVH your Elohim with all your heart and with all your soul. You shall walk after YHVH your Elohim, fear Him, keep His commandments, and obey His voice. You shall serve Him, and cling to Him. That prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death, because he has spoken rebellion against YHVH your Elohim, who brought you out of the land of Egypt and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, to draw you aside out of the way which YHVH your Elohim commanded you to walk in. So you shall remove the evil from among you.”
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 13:1–5

“But the prophet who presumes to speak a word in My Name that I have not commanded him to speak, or who speaks in the name of other gods, that same prophet shall die. And if you say in your heart, ‘How may we know the word that YHVH has not spoken?’ When a prophet speaks in the Name of YHVH, if the word does not come to pass or come true, that is a word that YHVH has not spoken; the prophet has spoken it presumptuously. You need not be afraid of him.”
- Devarim (Deuteronomy) 18:20–22

“Thus says YHVH of hosts: ‘Do not listen to the words of the prophets who prophesy to you. They make you worthless; they speak a vision of their own heart, not from the mouth of YHVH. They continually say to those who despise Me, “YHVH has said, ‘You shall have peace’”; and to everyone who walks according to the dictates of his own heart, they say, “No evil shall come upon you.”’ For who has stood in the counsel of YHVH, and has perceived and heard His word? Who has marked His word and heard it? Behold, the storm of YHVH has gone forth in fury, a whirling tempest; it will burst upon the head of the wicked. The anger of YHVH will not turn back until He has executed and accomplished the intents of His heart. In the latter days you will understand it clearly. I have not sent these prophets, yet they ran. I have not spoken to them, yet they prophesied. But if they had stood in My counsel, and had caused My people to hear My words, then they would have turned them from their evil way and from the evil of their doings.”
- Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 23:16–22

“Beware of false prophets, who come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves. By their fruits you will know them. Do you gather grapes from thorns or figs from thistles? Even so, every good tree produces good fruit, but the corrupt tree produces evil fruit. A good tree can’t produce evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree produce good fruit. Every tree that doesn’t grow good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire. Therefore by their fruits you will know them.”
- Mattityahu (Matthew) 7:15–20

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH commands His people not to follow those who speak falsely in His Name or lead them away from His instructions. A claim of divine authority does not make a message true. Neither persuasive speech, popularity, nor extraordinary signs gives anyone permission to contradict the Torah.

Devarim (Deuteronomy) 13:1–5 warns that even if a sign or wonder comes to pass, Yisra’el must not follow a messenger who urges them to turn away from YHVH. The message must be tested against covenant faithfulness. The question is not simply whether something impressive happens, but whether the teaching leads people to remain faithful to YHVH.

We must not surrender our discernment to a person who claims to speak for Elohim. We are responsible for testing what we hear and refusing teachings that contradict His instructions.

🩵 REFLECTION

False prophets may appear sincere, knowledgeable, compassionate, or impressive. Their presentation alone cannot establish the truth of their message. Yirmeyahu (Jeremiah) 23:16–22 warns against those who speak visions from their own hearts rather than from the mouth of YHVH. Their words may sound reassuring while failing to call people away from wrongdoing.

Yahusha HaMashiach also warned that false prophets may come in sheep’s clothing, but their fruit reveals what they are (Mattityahu 7:15–20). We must therefore examine both the teaching and the direction in which it leads. Does it encourage obedience, truth, humility, and accountability? Or does it excuse disobedience, elevate the messenger, and discourage honest examination?

Discernment does not mean accusing everyone who disagrees with us of being a false prophet. It means listening carefully, examining claims fairly, and refusing to follow words that lead us away from YHVH’s established instructions. No person’s title, confidence, popularity, or claimed spiritual gift places their words above the Torah.

Let us be willing to test every teaching without fear or favour. Listening carefully does not require unquestioning acceptance, and rejecting falsehood does not require hostility toward the person speaking. Our responsibility is to remain faithful to YHVH and His truth.

👐 PRAYER

YHVH, give us wisdom to recognise false teaching and courage to refuse words that lead us away from Your instructions. Guard us against fear, manipulation, and misplaced trust. Teach us to test what we hear with humility, to walk faithfully in Your ways, and to remain steadfast in truth.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.
