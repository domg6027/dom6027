📨 DAVAR LECHEM – Erev Shabbat, Shmini 12, 6028 AA

Shalom, Mitspacha!

⭐ THEME

Seeking guidance through lawful means

📘 READING

“Trust in YHVH with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths.”
- Mishlei (Proverbs) 3:5–6

“Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path.”
- Tehillim (Psalms) 119:105

“And if any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of Elohim, who gives to all generously and without reproach, and it shall be given to him.”
- Ya’aqob (James) 1:5

🟫 MITSVOT FOCUS

YHVH calls us to seek guidance through lawful means: His Word, prayer, wisdom, and righteous counsel.

We must not turn to divination, sorcery, or forbidden spiritual practices to discover what lies ahead.

Instead, we are to trust YHVH, test advice against His instructions, and wait patiently for His direction.

True guidance does not lead us away from obedience to His Word.

🩵 REFLECTION

Where do I turn first when I need guidance or wisdom?

Do I test the advice I receive against the instructions of YHVH?

Am I willing to wait patiently rather than seek forbidden ways of knowing what lies ahead?

👐 PRAYER

YHVH, our Elohim, guide us in Your truth and teach us to seek wisdom through the means You have established.

Guard our hearts against forbidden practices and the desire to know what You have not revealed.

Help us test every counsel against Your Word, walk in obedience, and wait patiently for Your direction.

As we enter Shabbat, let us rest in the assurance that You lead those who trust in You.

Baruch YHVH. Emet.

🕎 AARONITIC BLESSING

May YHVH bless you and guard you.
May YHVH make His face shine upon you and show you favour.
May YHVH lift up His face toward you and give you peace.
- Bemidbar (Numbers) 6:24–26
